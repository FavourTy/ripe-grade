How to take good photos
  • Use a plain white or light background: a plate, tray, paper or white cloth.
  • Shoot in daylight or shade. No flash. Avoid yellow bulbs.
  • Beans: spread one layer on a tray and shoot straight down.
  • Fruit: one photo from the side, one from above, one close-up of the stalk or stem.
  • Keep faces out of the photo.
